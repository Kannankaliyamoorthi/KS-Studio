Our Story

Crafting visual poetry since 2015

K S Phographt Studio is a boutique photography studio rooted in the belief that every image should move the viewer — not just inform them.

Founded by award-winning photographer Élise Laurent, our studio specialises in editorial portraiture, commercial campaigns, and documentary work that transcends the ordinary. We work with a select roster of brands, publications, and private clients to produce imagery that carries weight and feeling.

From intimate boudoir sessions to large-scale campaign productions, our philosophy remains constant: patience, light, and truth.
